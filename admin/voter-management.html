Import Voters

Fill the Excel or CSV template, then save as CSV UTF-8 in Excel before uploading. Up to 100 voters per file (256 KB). Keep phone numbers as text to preserve the leading zero; dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Leave Member ID and username blank to generate them.

Rows with errors will be skipped. Correct them in your file and upload them separately.
